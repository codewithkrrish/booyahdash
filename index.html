How to play

01

Drop in

You spawn in the middle of the arena with a pistol. A medkit and an SMG are already on the floor — grab them before the first wave arrives.

02

Clear the wave

Enemies spawn from the edges in escalating waves. Rushers chase you, brutes soak up bullets, gunners keep their distance and shoot back.

03

Respect the zone

The safe zone shrinks on a timer and drifts across the map. Outside it, your health ticks down. The minimap in the corner always shows where safe is.

04

Take the Booyah

Clear wave 10 and the run is yours. Feeling good? Keep going in endless mode and see how far the waves take you.

The arsenal

Weapons drop from fallen enemies and spawn on the floor. Every pickup replaces your current gun — when its ammo runs out, you fall back to the pistol.

Pistol

Your starting sidearm. Infinite ammo, honest damage, never lets you down when everything else runs dry.

34 dmg · medium fire rate · infinite ammo

SMG

Sprays fast at close range. Burns through its 120 rounds quickly, so make the first half of the magazine count.

17 dmg · very fast · 120 rounds

Scattergun

Six pellets per shot. Devastating up close, a polite suggestion at long range. Best paired with a dash.

24 dmg × 6 pellets · slow · 30 rounds

Longshot Rifle

One clean hit from across the arena. The longest range in the game and the best answer to gunners.

78 dmg · slow · 36 rounds · longest range

Know the enemy

Rusher

Fast, fragile, and always coming straight at you. They arrive in numbers from wave 1 — kite them, don't let them surround you.

Brute

Slow and heavy with a big health bar. Appears from wave 4. Save the rifle or the scattergun for these.

Gunner

Keeps its distance and shoots. Appears from wave 3. Break line of sight with a crate or close the gap fast.

Good to know

Does it need an install or an account?

No. Open index.html (or this page) and play. Scores are saved in your browser only — nothing leaves your device.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. Touch controls appear automatically: drag the left side of the screen to move, hold Fire to shoot with auto-aim, and tap Dash to dodge.

What happens after wave 10?

Clearing wave 10 wins the run and shows the Booyah screen. You can keep playing in endless mode — waves keep scaling and your score keeps climbing.

Is there sound?

Yes — all effects are synthesized in your browser with WebAudio, no audio files. Press M or use the Sound button to mute.
